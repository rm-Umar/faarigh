# فارغ Copy Guidelines — Philosophy-Aligned Language

## Core Principle
Inform and empower. Never shame, judge, or control.
The user is always in control. Every choice is valid — even choosing to proceed.

## ❌ NEVER USE:
- "Do you REALLY need to..." (passive aggressive guilt)
- "locked" / "blocked" / "restricted" (control language)
- "Your focus is steadfast/weak today" (judgment)
- "Productivity score" (moralizes about app categories)
- "The sanctuary remains undisturbed" (implies using phone = disturbing)
- "Threat categories" (treats content as enemy)
- "Shield" / "protection" / "defense" (militaristic framing)
- Any language implying apps/content are inherently bad
- Scores that create pressure to "be good"

## ✅ USE INSTEAD:

### Home Dashboard
- Greeting: "Good evening, Sahar." (just warm, no judgment)
- Instead of "wellbeing score": "Your day so far" — neutral data presentation
- Instead of "Your focus is steadfast": "Here's your day at a glance"
- Module cards: describe what they DO, not what they PREVENT
  - "App Pause — You've paused 4 times today" (neutral fact)
  - "Content Awareness — 2 check-ins today" (not "2 alerts" or "2 blocks")
  - "Screen Time — 3h 42m today" (just data, no judgment)

### Interventions (App Pause Overlay)
- Instead of "Do you really need to open Instagram?":
  "You're about to open Instagram. Take a moment."
- Instead of "Cancel" (implies they shouldn't):
  "Not right now" (their choice, no judgment)
- Instead of "Enter App" (grudging permission):
  "Open Instagram" (neutral, equal weight to both options)
- The breathing exercise is an OFFER, not a gate
- Timer text: "Opening in 5 seconds" (not "READY IN 5 SECONDS" which implies earning access)

### NSFW/Content Overlay
- Instead of "Content Filter Active" + shield:
  "Heads up — this content may be explicit."
- Instead of "We've applied a shield to maintain your digital sanctuary":
  "Would you like to continue? This is your choice."
- Instead of "Go Back" (primary) / "Continue anyway" (shameful):
  "Go back" and "Continue" — EQUAL visual weight. Neither is the "right" answer.
- NO shield imagery. Use a simple pause/awareness icon.
- Instead of "SAFETY PREFERENCES MANAGED IN SETTINGS":
  "You can adjust these preferences in Settings"

### Analytics/Stats
- Instead of "Threat Categories": "DNS Categories" or "Query Categories"
- Instead of "Blocked": "Filtered" (less aggressive)
- Instead of "Privacy Rate": "Filter Rate"
- Stats are presented as DATA, not report cards
- No green = good, red = bad coloring on personal stats
- "App Performance" → "App Usage" (performance implies judgment)

### Modules
- Instead of "Your Shield": "Your Modules" or "Your Toolkit"
- Modules are TOOLS, not guards or shields
- "Enable" / "Disable" (not "Activate protection")
- Module descriptions: what it does, not what it prevents

### Onboarding
- Instead of "Your Sanctuary. Secured.": "Your space. Your rules."
- Instead of "Three pillars of focus designed to restore your mental clarity":
  "Three tools to help you use your phone the way you want to"
- Permissions: honest, technical explanation. Not fear-based.
- Instead of "Continue to Sanctuary": "Continue"
- Goal setting: "These are starting points — adjust anytime"

### General
- The app is a MIRROR (shows you data) + TOOLKIT (gives you options)
- It is NOT a judge, guardian, parent, shield, or sanctuary-keeper
- All stats are neutral observations, never verdicts
- Both "I want to scroll mindlessly for 2 hours" and "I want to limit my usage" are equally valid choices
